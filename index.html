WHAT WAYSTONES DOES

A simple workspace for the part after “Apply.”

Keep the useful details together without maintaining another complicated system.

01

Extract job details

Paste a public job-posting URL and Waystones attempts to identify the company, role, location, salary, and source.

02

Review before saving

Parser results stay editable. You decide what gets stored, including status, date applied, notes, and corrections.

03

Keep a Sheet in sync

Waystones stores your application workspace in its database and maintains a synchronized Google Sheet for portability.

HOW IT WORKS

From job link to organized record.

Waystones is designed around a short, reviewable workflow rather than automatic applications or background submissions.

  1. Sign in with Google.Your Google account authenticates you and authorizes the spreadsheet connection.
  2. Paste a posting.Waystones reads the public page when the source allows it. Some job boards may block or limit parsing.
  3. Check the result.Correct anything that was incomplete or inaccurate before saving.
  4. Track your progress.Update application status, edit notes, remove records, and keep your Waystones-managed Sheet synchronized.

GOOGLE DATA ACCESS

Limited access, for a specific purpose.

Waystones requests the Google drive.file scope so it can create and update the spreadsheet it uses for your account. It does not request broad access to all files in your Google Drive.

AuthenticationBasic Google account information is used to sign you in and identify your Waystones account.
Spreadsheet syncYour saved application records are written to the Waystones spreadsheet created for your account.
Server-side connectionThe long-lived Google refresh credential used for sync is stored encrypted on the server rather than exposed in the page.
Your controlYou can remove applications, revoke Google access, and request deletion of your Waystones account data.
