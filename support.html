WAYSTONES SUPPORT

Support & Data Deletion

Get help with sign-in, job parsing, Google Sheets synchronization, account access, or deletion of data associated with your Waystones account.

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Contact support

Contact Waystones using the same email address you use to sign in whenever the request involves account access or deletion:

support@YOUR-DOMAIN.example

This placeholder must be replaced with your monitored production support email before Google OAuth verification.

Google sign-in

If sign-in fails, confirm that popups/redirects are allowed and that you are using the intended Google account. OAuth errors can also occur if the production domain or redirect URLs are not configured correctly.

Job parsing

Some job sites restrict automated access. You can still review and manually correct the extracted fields. For reliable application details, prefer the hiring company's own career page.

Google Sheets sync

If your Sheet stops synchronizing, sign out and reconnect Google if prompted. Do not remove or heavily restructure the managed sheet if you want automatic synchronization to continue.

Privacy questions

Review the Privacy Policy for the categories of information Waystones accesses, how Google data is used, and how credentials are stored.

Request deletion of Waystones account data

To request deletion, email the support address above from the same Google account used to sign in to Waystones and use the subject line Waystones Data Deletion Request.

After account ownership is verified, the deletion process should remove Waystones-side records associated with the account, including saved application records, account settings, and the stored Google connection credential.

A Google Sheet already created in your Google Drive is controlled by your Google account and is not automatically deleted when Waystones-side data is removed. Delete that file directly from Google Drive if you also want the spreadsheet removed.

Revoke Google access

You can revoke Waystones' Google authorization at any time from your Google Account's third-party connections page. Revoking access prevents future authorized Google API use but does not itself delete Waystones database records or files already stored in your Drive.

Delete an individual application

While signed in, use the delete control on the application row. Waystones deletes the database record and attempts to reconcile the synchronized Google Sheet. If Sheets is temporarily unavailable, verify the Sheet later or remove the row manually.
